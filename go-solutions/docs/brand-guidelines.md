# Go Solutions brand guidelines (website)

## Idea
A software firm that publishes its prices. The home page leads with a **rate card**: every product, its fixed price, in the visitor's currency. Everything else stays quiet so the prices and the burgundy do the talking.

## Colour
Burgundy is the house colour. Use it for primary buttons, the logo, the active tab, and the top edge of the rate card and buy box. Don't use it for errors (errors are rust, so the two never get confused).

| Role | Light | Dark | Contrast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Burgundy (fills, buttons) | `#7A1631` | `#B33552` | white text 10.6:1 / 5.9:1 |
| Burgundy (links, text) | `#7A1631` | `#EE9DB0` | 9.7:1 / 9.1:1 on page |
| Page background | `#F6F4F4` | `#141012` | |
| Text | `#1C1719` | `#F2EDEE` | 16.2:1 / 16.3:1 |
| Secondary text | `#675B60` | `#B4A7AB` | 5.9:1 / 8.1:1 |
| Closing band | `#34101A` | `#2A0C15` | white 17:1 |
| Success / warning / error | `#1F7350` / `#8A5300` / `#A8360F` | `#5CC79A` / `#F0B35A` / `#F29573` | all above 4.5:1 on their tints |

All text passes WCAG AA in both light and dark mode.

## Type
- **Archivo** (wide, 116%, weights 700 to 800) for headings, the logo and big prices only.
- **Public Sans** (400 to 700) for everything else. Numbers use tabular figures so prices line up.
- Monospace only for order references like `GS7K2M9Q`.

## Logo
A burgundy rounded square with "go" in wide Archivo, next to "Go Solutions" in Archivo. Swap in Simon's real logo when he has one.

## Voice
Plain and specific. Say what something costs, what's included and when it arrives. No buzzwords, no hype, no exclamation marks except "Thank you!" after a payment. Write for a busy business owner reading in their second language: short sentences, common words.
